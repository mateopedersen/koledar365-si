# City day and night photographs, 13 November 2026

15 of 69 selectable cities have a distinct source-verified night photograph. The existing 69 day photographs remain intact. No daylight image has been artificially darkened. Cities without an accepted night photograph keep their existing labelled photograph; they are not claimed as complete.

The selected place and its astronomical sunrise/set control the photograph independently of forecast latency. Decoded image caching, stale-response guards, an exact next-event timer and foreground refresh prevent blank frames and crossed city labels. Source, author and licence are recorded per image.

Validation completed locally: 552 selections over Chrome/WebKit, mobile/desktop and day/night; four race scenarios; 32 existing browser regressions; six accessibility/overflow views without violations. The complete publication gates still run in the normal GitHub release workflow. Accepted calendar downloads were not regenerated.
