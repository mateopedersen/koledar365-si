# Internal linking

Baseline: 391 public pages reachable from the homepage, no orphans, maximum shortest-path depth two. Existing complete content directory and the PR #58 month pager are retained.

Added contextual links connect:

- A calendar month to the same lunar/workday month, holiday year, parent year and editable monthly planner.
- A monthly workday page to its annual comparison and a calculator prefilled with that month's actual boundaries.
- Long weekends to the same holiday/workday year, holiday-planning tool and verified school holidays.
- ISO-week/day-of-year tools to weekly planning, date differences and leap-year rules.
- Editorial policy to About, sources, contact and privacy.

The machine-readable graph records edges, incoming pages and shortest paths. Static checks also validate cross-page fragment identifiers. Links remain native anchors; no hidden keyword/link blocks were introduced.
