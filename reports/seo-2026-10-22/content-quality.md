# Content and Slovene review

The URL inventory retains a unique title, description, H1, canonical and classification for every exported page. Existing good metadata is preserved. Review combines all-page structural checks, period/data tests, template review and inspection of exceptions; it is not external native-speaker certification.

Specific improvements:

- Annual/monthly calendar pages explain actual period length, first/last weekdays, ISO-week boundaries, workday assumptions, observed holidays and printing/editing options. February 2028 is explicitly tested.
- Monthly workday pages distinguish a one-month fund from an annual month-by-month overview; their descriptions now match the actual page. Links open the same calendar month and prefilled calculator.
- ISO-week and day-of-year tools explain inclusivity, leap days, ISO-year transitions and the Ljubljana date, with dated numerical examples.
- Long-weekend pages explain total rest versus leave used, limited result count, work schedule assumptions and approval limits.
- Editorial policy gives a real source/correction process and does not invent experts, qualifications or outside language review.
- The family-planner description reflects events, tasks, members, shopping, local storage and ICS instead of focusing only on a generic print download.
- Existing education pages retain official source/status distinctions. Unpublished future school years remain explicitly held and excluded. Payment schedules remain limited to the verified published coverage. Horoscope content remains entertainment.

Slovene changes include explicit “mesečni fond”, “zaporedna številka dneva”, “leto ISO”, natural calendar instructions and period-specific sentences. Original official names and source titles are retained. Month names in prose remain lowercase where Slovene grammar requires it; standalone month labels retain the site's chosen initial capital.

Search-intent mapping covers every canonical URL in `current/search-intent.csv`; it records the actual topic, metadata, heading outline, schema and related destinations. Search results were inspected for calendar, lunar, workday, printing and date-tool phrases. No search volume, CTR, rank or Search Console performance number was invented.
